    Guide 11 · Account admin

    Roles & Permissions

    A role decides what each member can see and change inside the workspace. This guide covers how permissions work, the three built-in roles, custom roles built from the five templates, and how to assign them without opening doors you do not need.

    ⚠️

    The Roles & permissions and Team screens are available to account administrators only. A role never unlocks a feature your plan does not include; it decides what a member can do with the features you have.

    1. How permissions work

    Every role is built from permissions on a module, with exactly two actions:

    • View: open the screen, read its data and export what the screen allows.
    • Create & edit: add, edit and delete inside the module.

    The permission is checked on the server for every request, based on the route — hiding a button is only a convenience on top of that check. A route that belongs to no known module is denied automatically, and an unknown or deleted role grants nothing.

    Plan versus role

    The plan decides which features the workspace has at all (member count, channels, AI, the API…). The role decides what a member can do within what is available. For example: if the plan does not include the API, no role can open it. If it does, only a role with Workspace settings can open it.

    2. The three built-in roles

    Open Settings → Team → Roles & permissions (or use the Roles & permissions button at the top of the Team page). The built-in roles appear first with a Built-in badge, followed by the roles your workspace created.

    RolePermissionsCannot
    AdministratorEvery module and action, including team, billing, the audit log and privacyNothing inside the workspace
    StaffDaily work across all modules: conversations, contacts, leads, sales, marketing, automation, reportsTeam and roles, audit log, privacy and GDPR
    ViewerView and reports across all modules, with no editingCreating, editing and deleting, plus team, audit log and privacy

    Built-in roles cannot be edited or deleted: the system relies on them, especially for last-administrator protection. Need different behaviour? Create a custom role and move the members to it.

    💡

    Most teams can start with: Administrator for the owner or operations lead, Staff for sales and support, and Viewer for people who only follow along. Add a custom role when someone needs something different.

    3. Create a custom role

    1. On the Roles & permissions page, click Add role.
    2. Pick a template (see the next section) or start from scratch.
    3. Give it a clear, role-based name such as “Sales supervisor” and a short description that helps the other administrators.
    4. For each module, tick View and/or Create & edit. Ticking “Create & edit” also ticks “View”; unticking “View” also removes edit.
    5. Administrators only modules are shown disabled and cannot be granted to a custom role: team and roles, audit log, privacy and GDPR.
    6. Save. The role appears in every role picker immediately, with no sign-out required.

    Modules you can grant

    AreaModules
    InboxInbox (conversations, replies and labels)
    ContactsContacts & segments
    SalesLeads & tasks · Quotes & products · Invoices · Bookings & forms · Call log · Sequences · AI SDR · E-commerce
    MarketingCampaigns · WhatsApp marketing · SMS gateways · Email server · Social media
    AutomationAutomations
    ReportsReports & exports
    SettingsAI settings · Workspace settings (including the API, webhooks and support) · Billing & subscription · Data export · Team & roles * · Audit log * · Privacy & GDPR *

    * Administrators only. They stay in the matrix because the Administrator role owns them, but they are disabled for every custom role.

    Custom roles are stored per workspace; another workspace cannot see your roles or assign them to its members.

    4. The five templates

    Templates are a starting point, not roles you use as-is: choosing one copies its permissions into the new role, and you can adjust them freely. Editing the role later changes neither the template nor any other role.

    TemplateWhat it grants by default
    SupervisorAlmost every module except billing, privacy and the audit log. Suited to a team lead who needs the daily work without the owner’s financial powers.
    Sales agentInbox, contacts, leads, quotes, bookings, calls and sequences (view and edit), plus view access to invoices and reports, and workspace settings.
    Support agentInbox and contacts (view and edit), view access to leads and bookings, and workspace settings. No campaigns or billing.
    MarketerView contacts; edit campaigns, WhatsApp marketing, SMS, the email server, social media and automation; view reports and workspace settings.
    AccountantInvoices (view and edit), view access to quotes, reports and billing, plus data export. No conversations, campaigns or team.
    ⚠️

    The Accountant template only grants view access to billing. Upgrading or downgrading the plan, or cancelling the subscription, requires Create & edit on billing — grant it only to whoever actually manages payment.

    5. Assign a role to team members

    From Settings → Team:

    • When using Invite by email or Add member, choose the role before sending or saving (Staff is the default).
    • To change an existing member, click the pencil icon next to them, pick the role and save.
    • The role appears as a badge next to every member in the team table, and the new permissions apply to their very next request.
    • Pending invitations keep the chosen role until the member accepts or the invitation is revoked.
    💡

    Before changing a member’s role, reassign their open conversations, tasks and deals to a colleague. Changing the role keeps historical data, but it may lock that member out of it the next day.

    6. Edit or delete a role

    • Edit: the pencil icon on a custom role. Change the name, description or permissions, and the change applies immediately to everyone holding the role.
    • Delete: the trash icon. A role that is still in use cannot be deleted; reassign its members to another role first. The button is disabled and explains why on hover.
    • Built-in roles have no edit or delete buttons. When you need different behaviour, create a custom role and move the members to it.

    7. Keeping the account safe

    • There must always be at least one administrator: the last Administrator cannot be removed or demoted, so administration never locks everyone out.
    • Grant Administrator only to people who genuinely manage the team and billing.
    • Review roles regularly: someone who moved from sales to accounting should move to the matching role instead of keeping their old permissions.
    • The audit log and privacy stay with administrators, so a custom role cannot erase records or run erasure requests.

    8. FAQ

    QuestionAnswer
    Can a custom role manage the team or roles?No. Team, audit log and privacy are “administrators only” modules and stay with the account administrator.
    Can I give the accountant billing access?Yes: the Accountant template grants view access to billing. Changing or cancelling the subscription requires Create & edit on billing.
    Why can I not see a screen my role allows?Check the plan first (is the feature included?), then the current workspace. A permission never exceeds the plan.
    What if a role is deleted while still in use?The UI prevents it. If it happens outside the UI, the member loses all permissions and is denied by default until a valid role is assigned.
    Are roles per workspace?Yes. Every workspace owns its custom roles, and a role from another workspace can never be used.
    Do role permissions cover API keys?No. API keys have their own scopes and are managed from workspace settings.
    Does hiding a screen protect the data?The server checks the permission on every request, so hiding buttons and links is not the only protection — but still avoid sharing data a role does not need.

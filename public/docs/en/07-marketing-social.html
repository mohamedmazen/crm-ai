    Marketing, Campaigns & Social

    Every tool for reaching your customer in one place: WhatsApp, SMS and email campaigns, templates, auto-replies, the chat widget, social posting, forms, and booking pages.

    1. Campaigns

    From Marketing → Campaigns. A campaign sends one message to a defined list over a single channel.

    Creating a campaign

    1. Click New Campaign and choose the channel: WhatsApp, SMS, or Email.
    2. Choose the audience: all contacts, a segment, a tag, or a custom list.
    3. Design the content per channel:
      • WhatsApp: choose an approved template and the sending number.
      • SMS: write the text (and check the character count and balance).
      • Email: subject, body, From, and Reply-To.
    4. Review the preview and the expected audience.
    5. Send now, or schedule the campaign for a later time.
    ⚠️

    On WhatsApp you cannot start a conversation with free text. You need an approved template, otherwise Meta will reject the campaign. Prepare the template a day before the campaign.

    Tracking a campaign

    On the campaign page you see: the number targeted, sent, failed, and read (per channel), with a detailed report and an export of the recipients.

    Campaign fields reference

    A campaign is defined by who receives it, what they receive and when. The totals columns update as recipients are processed.

    FieldWhat it means
    NameInternal label for the campaign.
    ChannelWhatsApp, SMS or email.
    AudienceA segment, a tag filter or a manually selected list.
    Template / payloadThe approved WhatsApp template or the email/SMS body.
    ScheduleSend immediately or at a specific date and time.
    TimezoneThe timezone the schedule is expressed in.
    StatusDraft, scheduled, sending, completed, paused or failed.
    RecipientsTotal contacts targeted after duplicates and opt-outs are removed.
    Delivered / read / repliedEngagement counters shown in the campaign report.

    2. WhatsApp Templates

    From Marketing → WhatsApp Templates.

    1. Click New Template and choose the category (Marketing / Utility / Authentication).
    2. Write the header, body, footer, and buttons.
    3. Add variables such as {{1}} for the name.
    4. Submit it to Meta for approval.
    5. Check the status: approved / pending review / rejected.

    After linking, you can sync the templates from the channel setup page to pull all your WABA templates.

    3. Auto Replies

    From Marketing → Auto Replies. An instant reply to keywords with no human intervention.

    1. Create a new reply.
    2. Define the trigger words or phrases.
    3. Write the reply (text or template).
    4. Enable it. You can disable it at any time without deleting it.
    💡

    Use auto-replies for frequently asked questions (prices, opening hours, address). For complex automation, use Automation.

    4. Chat Widget

    A chat button embedded on your website that opens WhatsApp or a form. From Marketing → Chat Widget.

    1. Create a widget and choose the channel and number.
    2. Customize the text, color, and position.
    3. Copy the embed code and place it on your site before </body>.
    4. Use the preview button to confirm it looks right.

    5. SMS & Email

    Configuration is done from:

    • Marketing → SMS Gateways — choose the provider and enter the keys and the Sender ID.
    • Marketing → Mail Server — SMTP and From.

    Step-by-step details are in the Integrations guide.

    6. Social Media

    Connecting accounts

    From Marketing → Social Accounts. Supported networks:

    NetworkNote
    FacebookA page managed by your account
    InstagramA business account linked to a Facebook page
    LinkedInA personal account or a company page, depending on permissions
    X (Twitter)OAuth sign-in
    YouTubeUpload/post a video
    TikTokPost a video
    1. Click Connect Account and choose the network.
    2. Sign in and approve the permissions.
    3. Choose the page/account.
    4. Make sure the status shows “Connected”.

    Post Composer

    1. Write the text.
    2. Add an image or a video.
    3. Choose the networks (you can post to more than one network).
    4. Preview the content for each network — the limits differ (for example, X is shorter).
    5. Publish now or schedule.

    Posts & Calendar

    • Posts: drafts, scheduled, published, and failed.
    • Calendar: a monthly view of everything to be published.
    • AI Planner: suggests ideas and posting times (consumes tokens).
    • A failed post shows the reason — often an expired network token; reconnect the account.

    7. Forms

    From Marketing → Forms (the Forms feature).

    1. Create a form and open the builder.
    2. Add fields (text, choice, email, phone...).
    3. Link the form to a Lead Source to track the campaign.
    4. Save and publish — it becomes available at /f/{slug}.
    5. Embed it on your site with the Embed code if you want.

    Every submission creates a lead and appears in form analytics. The number of forms and submissions is limited by your plan.

    8. Booking Pages

    From Marketing → Booking (the Booking feature).

    1. Create a meeting type: duration, description, price if needed.
    2. Set the availability times and the time zone.
    3. Share the booking link /b/{slug}.
    4. The customer picks a slot and confirms.
    5. They can reschedule or cancel from the confirmation link.

    Bookings appear on the same screen, and a booking can be linked to an automation trigger or send a reminder.

    9. Lead Sources

    From Marketing → Lead Sources. Create a source (such as “Facebook campaign”) and get a UTM link/token to mark the leads coming from it. The Attribution report uses these to identify the best channel.

    Common mistakes

    ProblemCauseFix
    The campaign does not sendTemplate not approved or channel not connectedCheck the template and the channel status
    Recipient count is zeroThe segment/tag contains no oneReview the segment or choose another audience
    Social post failedExpired token or missing permissionReconnect the account and grant the permissions
    The widget does not appearThe code is in the wrong place or CSPPlace it before </body> and check the site policy
    A form does not create leadsThe lead source or the required fieldsCheck the form settings and the Lead Source
    “Reached the message limit”The monthly limit was exceededPrevious messages keep working, but new sends stop — upgrade your plan

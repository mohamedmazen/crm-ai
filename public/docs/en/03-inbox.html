    Inbox & Conversations

    The inbox is your daily workplace: every message from every channel arrives here in a single thread. Learn the folders, canned replies, labels and assignment, and the chaos will disappear.

    💡

    Before you start, make sure the channels are actually connected: Setup & Integrations guide.

    Folders

    On the right of the screen (or at the top of the list on mobile) you will find the following folders:

    FolderWhat it shows
    AllAll active conversations
    MineConversations assigned to you
    UnassignedConversations nobody has picked up — keep an eye on them constantly
    ResolvedConversations closed after being resolved
    SnoozedConversations you postponed to a later time

    You can also filter by channel (WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, SMS, email) or by account or by label.

    Reading a conversation and replying

    1. Choose the conversation from the left-hand list.
    2. Read the previous messages — all channels appear in the same thread per customer.
    3. Write your reply in the box at the bottom of the screen. It supports text and media (image/file/voice) depending on the channel.
    4. Click send. The delivery status (sent/delivered/read) appears on WhatsApp.

    The 24-hour rule on WhatsApp

    WhatsApp allows a free-form reply within 24 hours of the customer’s last message. After the window closes, you must send an approved template. If you try to reply with free text after the period ends, WhatsApp will reject the message — use the “Send template” button.

    Canned Replies

    Repeated replies inserted with a single click. Create them from Inbox ← Canned replies.

    1. Click “Add reply”.
    2. Enter a short title (visible only to you) and the reply text.
    3. Variables such as the customer’s name can be used if they are supported in the text editor.
    4. Save. When writing in a conversation, type / or open the replies list to insert it.

    Labels

    A powerful organization tool: “VIP customer”, “Awaiting payment”, “Shipping issue”... Create them from Inbox ← Labels with a distinctive color.

    • Add more than one label to the same conversation.
    • Use the label as a filter to display all of its conversations.
    • A label can be linked to an automation trigger (for example: when the “Interested” label is added, send a follow-up message).

    Assignment

    • Assign the conversation to yourself or to a colleague from the assignment list.
    • Unassigned conversations appear in the “Unassigned” folder — distribute them at the start of the day.
    • Every change in assignment or labels is recorded in the conversation activity log.

    Converting a conversation to a Lead

    When the customer becomes a sales opportunity, convert them to a lead in one click:

    1. Open the conversation.
    2. Click Convert to Lead.
    3. Review the name, number and email, and add any missing details.
    4. Save — the lead is created and appears in the pipeline.

    Sharing a product inside the conversation

    If you have connected your store (Shopify/WooCommerce), you can send a product card directly:

    1. From the conversation, click Share product.
    2. Search for the product and select it.
    3. The product link is sent with its image and price in a single message.

    Ending a conversation

    ActionWhen to use itResult
    ResolveThe need is finishedIt moves to the “Resolved” folder
    SnoozeWaiting for the customer’s reply or for informationIt disappears temporarily and returns at the set time
    ReopenThe customer replies after resolutionIt returns to the active folder automatically

    Notifications

    • In-app notifications (the bell at the top of the screen).
    • Browser notifications (Web Push) — enable them from Settings ← Notifications.
    • Alert sounds for a new conversation can be turned on/off from the inbox.

    Common mistakes

    ProblemCauseSolution
    The send button is disabledThe conversation is resolved or the channel is disconnectedReopen the conversation and make sure the channel connection is healthy
    WhatsApp message rejectedThe 24-hour window endedSend an approved template instead of free text
    New conversations do not appearA channel/label filter is activeGo back to the “All” folder and clear the filters
    A colleague cannot see the conversationIt is assigned to you or it is in another workspaceReassign it or switch workspaces
    Media is not sentThe size/format is unsupported on the channelUse a smaller format or an external link
    ✅

    Operational tip: start your day with the “Unassigned” folder, then “Mine”, and close the resolved conversations. This prevents work from piling up.

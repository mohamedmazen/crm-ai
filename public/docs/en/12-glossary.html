    Glossary

    The words AYDRA uses on screen, in this manual and in support conversations — defined once so the rest of the guides stay short. Use Ctrl+K to jump straight to a term.

    💡

    Arabic readers: every term below has a matching entry with the Arabic label in the Arabic glossary.

    Account and workspace

    TermMeaning
    TenantThe customer account that owns the data. One tenant can hold several workspaces, and tenants never see each other’s records.
    WorkspaceThe isolated container you work inside: contacts, conversations, leads, campaigns and settings. Switch between workspaces from Settings → Workspaces.
    RoleAdministrator, Staff or Viewer — or a custom role your workspace creates. The role decides which screens and actions are available. See the Roles & Permissions guide.
    PlanThe subscription tier: Free, Starter, Pro or Business. It sets numeric limits and unlocks features.
    TrialA 60-day period with Business-level limits and every feature unlocked.
    Soft limitOver-limit policy: existing data is never deleted — only the creation of new records is blocked until you upgrade.
    White-labelReplacing AYDRA branding with your own logo and colours. Available on higher plans.

    Channels and WhatsApp

    TermMeaning
    ChannelA connected messaging account: WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, SMS or email.
    WABAWhatsApp Business Account — the Meta container that holds your WhatsApp number and templates.
    Embedded SignupThe Meta flow that connects a WhatsApp number to AYDRA without leaving the setup screen.
    CoexistenceRunning your existing WhatsApp Business app and AYDRA on the same number at the same time. See guide 11.
    TemplateA pre-approved WhatsApp message with placeholders. Marketing templates need opt-in.
    Opt-inThe customer’s explicit consent to receive messages from you. Required for marketing sends.
    BYOKBring Your Own Keys: you supply the WhatsApp, SMS, email and AI credentials. AYDRA never silently falls back to platform-owned credentials.
    WebhookAn outbound HTTP call AYDRA makes when an event happens, for example a new message or a won deal.

    Inbox and contacts

    TermMeaning
    ConversationA thread with one contact on one channel. It groups every inbound and outbound message.
    Canned replyA reusable message snippet inserted with a shortcut.
    LabelA tag attached to a conversation for filtering and reporting.
    ContactA person or customer record: name, phone, email, tags and custom fields.
    SegmentA saved group of contacts. Static segments are fixed lists; dynamic segments update from rules.
    Custom fieldAn extra contact attribute you define, such as “Favourite class” or “Contract renewal date”.

    Leads and pipeline

    TermMeaning
    LeadA sales opportunity — a person or company you are trying to win.
    PipelineAn ordered set of stages that represents your sales process.
    StageOne column of the pipeline, such as New, Contacted or Won.
    ScoreAn automatic quality number calculated from behaviour, source and engagement.
    CompanyAn organisation record linked to contacts and leads.
    TaskA follow-up to-do with an owner, priority and due date.
    Call logThe record of inbound and outbound calls, including duration and notes.

    Sales and billing

    TermMeaning
    ProductA catalogue item with a price, SKU, tax rate and unit.
    QuoteA priced proposal sent to a lead, with a public link and a validity date.
    InvoiceA request for payment generated from a quote or manually.
    SequenceAn automated series of email or WhatsApp steps with delays and stop conditions.
    CampaignA bulk send to an audience on WhatsApp, SMS or email, with delivery statistics.
    BroadcastA one-off mass message; campaigns are the managed version of it.
    AudienceThe contacts a campaign or sequence targets — usually a segment.

    Marketing, automation and AI

    TermMeaning
    Auto-replyA rule that answers automatically: keyword, welcome, away or out-of-hours.
    Chat widgetThe chat bubble you embed on your website.
    Social postContent scheduled or published to connected social accounts.
    FormA public capture form that creates contacts or leads when submitted.
    Meeting typeA bookable appointment definition: duration, buffers, location and questions.
    AutomationA workflow made of one trigger and one or more actions.
    TriggerThe event that starts an automation, such as contact.created or lead.won.
    RunOne execution of an automation for one record.
    Knowledge baseDocuments and answers the AI searches before replying.
    ChatbotAn AI agent configured with a prompt, a knowledge base and a fallback reply.
    AI SDRAn AI sales development rep that researches and qualifies leads.

    Technical and security

    TermMeaning
    API tokenA secret key that lets an external system call the AYDRA API on your behalf.
    Audit logA record of sensitive actions: who did what, when, and from where.
    2FATwo-factor authentication: a second code required at sign-in.
    RTLRight-to-left layout, used for Arabic pages and messages.
    StorageThe file and media space counted against your plan.
